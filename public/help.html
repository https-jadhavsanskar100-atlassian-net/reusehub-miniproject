Help & safety

Trade thoughtfully.

Simple habits help keep student-to-student exchanges safer.

Meet in public

Choose a busy campus location in daylight. Avoid isolated locations and do not invite strangers to private rooms.

Inspect before agreeing

Check the item's condition and agree on any price or exchange terms before completing the handover.

Protect your details

Never share passwords, one-time codes, bank PINs or unnecessary personal information in messages.

Spot suspicious listings

Be careful with unrealistic offers, pressure to pay in advance, or requests to move to unfamiliar links.

Payments happen offline

ReUseHub currently supports enquiries and exchange coordination. It does not provide checkout, escrow or shipping.

Report concerns

Use the report button on a listing to flag misleading or suspicious content for review.
