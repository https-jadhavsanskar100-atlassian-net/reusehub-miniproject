Privacy notice.

Project privacy summary — confirm your hosting and retention practices before real-world use.

Information used

The app may process account identity, profile details you provide, listings, favourites, enquiries, messages, notifications and reports.

Why information is used

Data supports marketplace listings, student conversations, saved items, updates, abuse prevention and moderation.

Visibility

Listings are visible to marketplace visitors. Messages and account-specific records should be restricted to relevant participants. Avoid sensitive personal information in listings and messages.

External services

Book search may query Open Library. Hosting and authentication are provided by the app platform. Review those providers' terms and privacy documentation.

Retention and account requests

A production release should define deletion, export, retention and contact procedures that comply with applicable law and institutional policy.

Important

This is a project privacy template, not legal advice. Update it to reflect actual processing, retention periods, contact details and the applicable institution before collecting real student data.
