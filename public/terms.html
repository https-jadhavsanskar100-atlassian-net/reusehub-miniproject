The basics

Terms of use.

Draft terms for the student project. Review them before a real public launch.

1. Marketplace role

ReUseHub helps students discover listings and communicate about potential exchanges. It does not process payments, provide escrow, arrange shipping or guarantee a transaction.

2. Accurate listings

Users must provide honest descriptions, accurate condition information and valid pricing. Listings must concern lawful, permitted goods. Do not list stolen, dangerous, prohibited or counterfeit items.

3. User conduct

Users must not harass others, impersonate students, misuse messages, manipulate listings or access another person's account or data.

4. Exchanges and safety

Users are responsible for checking items and agreeing on safe handovers. Meet in public places, use good judgment, and do not share passwords or verification codes.

5. Moderation

Listings may be reviewed, hidden or removed when reported or when they appear to violate these rules.

6. Availability

This student project is provided as-is for demonstration. Features may change and uninterrupted availability is not guaranteed.
